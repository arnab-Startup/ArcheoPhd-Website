The PhD Problem

Archaeology research doesn't fail from a lack of data — it fails from a lack of connection.

Scattered sources

Hundreds of PDFs, excavation reports and field notes live in unrelated folders with no connections between them.

Disconnected evidence

A claim in chapter three may rely on evidence buried in a report you read eight months ago — and forgot.

Chronology conflicts

Different publications date the same site differently. These disagreements are rarely surfaced together.

4.3 The Radiocarbon Horizon of Stratum VIA

The chronological attribution of Stratum VIA at Tell Megiddo represents a fundamental crux in Levantine Iron Age archaeological sequencing. While traditional scholarship correlated the destruction layer with military incursions under Pharaoh Shoshenq I, subsequent AMS radiometric dating campaigns of short-lived charred olive pits demonstrate a clustering around the late eleventh and early tenth centuries BCE[14].

Sparse Strata

  • Mitathal: sporadic surface survey mentions without stratigraphic context or radiocarbon dating.

Underrepresented Periods

Chronology Gap
  • 1900–1600 BCE transition hiatus: radiocarbon gap between mature urban phase and regional agrarian settlements.
  • Early Ochre Coloured Pottery (OCP) stratigraphic overlap with Late Copper Hoards.

Contradictory Interpretations

Active Scholarly Dispute
  • Chronological Conflict: Lal (1955) assigns Hastinapura Period II to 1100–800 BCE via typo-chronology, whereas modern calibrated radiocarbon series (Possehl 2002) dates equivalent strata to 1250–950 BCE.
