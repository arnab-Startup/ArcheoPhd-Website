Everything you need to know about app size, offline neural reasoning, and scholarly privacy.

The application engine (ArchaeoPhD.exe) is written in native C++ and is only 9.52 MB. This architecture ensures you can download the 9.5 MB installer instantly and choose any drive (e.g. an external SSD or D: drive) to store the heavy models.
